================================
RECOMMENDED FREE FIRE SETUP
================================

1. DISPLAY & PERFORMANCE

• Use the highest stable FPS option available in Free Fire.
• Choose graphics settings that keep gameplay smooth.
• Avoid settings that cause frequent frame drops.
• Keep enough free storage space on your iPhone.

2. NETWORK

• Install the BOLO AIM DNS profile.
• Use a stable Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
• Avoid large downloads while playing.
• If your connection becomes unstable, test with and without
  the DNS profile to see which works better on your network.

3. FREE FIRE

• Select your preferred sensitivity preset.
• Enter the values manually inside Free Fire.
• Test your settings in Training Mode.
• Make small adjustments based on your play style.

4. BEFORE PLAYING

• Make sure your connection is stable.
• Close unnecessary high-usage apps.
• Make sure Free Fire is updated.
• Restart the game if you experience unusual performance issues.

================================
IMPORTANT
================================

BOLO AIM does not modify Free Fire.
BOLO AIM does not provide automatic aiming or headshots.

The DNS profile changes DNS resolution and does not directly
change sensitivity, recoil, damage, or hit registration.
