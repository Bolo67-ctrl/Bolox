================================
HOW TO USE
================================

1. Open Free Fire.
2. Go to Settings → Sensitivity.
3. Enter your chosen preset manually.
4. Test it in Training Mode.
5. Adjust the values slightly until they feel comfortable
   on your specific iPhone.

These are starting-point sensitivity presets.
They do not provide automatic aim or headshots.

BOLO AIM DNS does not change these sensitivity values.
The DNS and sensitivity presets are separate parts of the
BOLO AIM setup.
